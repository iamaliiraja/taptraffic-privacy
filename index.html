Tap Traffic — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 June 2026

Tap Traffic (“the app”) is a casual driving game. We care about your privacy and keep things simple.

What we collect

No personal information. The app does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personally identifiable information.

  • Game progress (your level, coins, unlocked cars, best distance, and settings) is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, or photos.

Advertising

The app displays ads via Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as an advertising ID) and usage data, in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy. You can opt out of ad personalization in your device settings. No other third parties receive your data.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
