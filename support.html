Tap Traffic — Support

Thanks for playing Tap Traffic! Tap & hold to drive, release to stop, and time your run through the traffic.

How to play

  • Hold anywhere on the screen to drive; release to brake.
  • Time your run to cross intersections, highways and railroad crossings without crashing.
  • Tap HONK to briefly slow nearby traffic.
  • Collect coins to unlock new cars in the Garage; grab shields, magnets and ramps along the way.

Common questions

My progress reset / I changed phones. Progress is saved locally on your device, so it doesn't transfer between devices or reinstalls.

No sound? Check Settings → Sound is ON, and that your device isn't muted. Audio starts after your first tap.

Remove ads. Use Settings → Remove Ads.
